# Program source audit

Checked November 6, 2026 for Fall 2027 entry. Selected manually from the first two alphabetical pages of [AMS doctoral listings](https://www.ams.org/find-graduate-programs?prog_type=PHD), excluding standalone statistics, health, and unrelated doctoral records. This is a small initial catalogue, not a ranking or recommendation. Brown and Cornell each contribute two distinct programs. Boise is the Computing PhD mathematical emphasis named in the AMS department record.

AMS supplies institution/location and selected research specialties. Official university pages supply degree names and admissions dates. Research tags are a small normalized subset, not a complete research inventory; the AMS combined Algebra/Number Theory category remains combined. Claremont and Boise tags use university program descriptions. No tuition, acceptance rate, funding guarantee, GRE policy, or comprehensive requirements are inferred.

Nine records have dates explicitly identifying this application cycle. Thirteen publish annual dates without a cycle year; two have rolling admission; one has conflicting dates. Only the nine cycle-confirmed dates populate new tracker entries. Other entries require a user planning date. The directory always preserves published information separately from editable plans.

Sources may change after this check. Annual dates are reported without inventing a year. Funding priority, recommended dates, and final deadlines are distinguished in the notes. When a source names no cutoff time, none is invented.

## Verification notes

### Auburn University — Mathematics

The department accepts applications year-round and recommends fall entry for teaching assistantships. Graduate School materials are due at least 45 days before classes begin; confirm the funding timeline with the department.

### Baylor University — Mathematics

The department publishes December 15 without an application year. Fall 2027 needs confirmation. The department accepts PhD applicants only.

### Binghamton University — Mathematical Sciences

Annual policy: January 30 for funding; applications received by April 15 receive full admission consideration. Neither source explicitly assigns the dates to Fall 2027.

### Boise State University — Computing — Computational Mathematics, Science & Engineering

A Computing PhD emphasis, not a standalone Mathematics PhD. Annual deadlines: January 5 priority admission/funding; April 15 final. The page does not name the 2027 entry year.

### Boston College — Mathematics

Department and graduate admissions agree on January 20 for full consideration, but do not explicitly identify Fall 2027.

### Boston University — Mathematics

The Graduate School explicitly lists January 11 under Mathematics (PhD) for Fall 2027. The engineering deadline is separate.

### Bowling Green State University — Mathematics

Rolling applications; apply by January 31 for full fall funding consideration. This is not a final cutoff; the year is unspecified.

### Brigham Young University — Mathematics

January 15 for spring, summer, and fall; late applications may be considered. Fall 2027 is not explicitly named. An ecclesiastical endorsement is required.

### Brown University — Mathematics

Current application explorer: January 1, 2027. The department confirms Fall 2027 admissions and describes a new short mathematical video requirement.

### Brown University — Applied Mathematics

The graduate program page and current application explorer agree on December 9, 2026. Separate from Brown Mathematics.

### Bryn Mawr College — Mathematics

Fall 2027 application page: January 15, 2027 for Mathematics. Exceptional late applications with financial support may be considered.

### California Institute of Technology — Mathematics

December 10 for the following academic year; Fall 2027 is not named. Research filters remain broad because AMS specialty fields are blank.

### Carnegie Mellon University — Mathematical Sciences

All materials by December 20 for assured full consideration. Later applications may not be reviewed. No application year is stated.

### Case Western Reserve University — Mathematics

Graduate School: PhD priority January 5, 2027; final May 10, 2027. MS priority is March 1, a different date.

### Central Michigan University — Mathematical Sciences

February 15 admission/support date; later applications considered while positions remain. Fall 2027 start is shown, but the deadline itself has no explicit year.

### Claremont Graduate University — Mathematics

Fall 2027 priority February 1; final international June 1 and final domestic August 1. General admissions corroborates these dates.

### Clarkson University — Mathematics

Applications accepted at any time for fall/spring, at least two months before the semester. Assistantships have additional timelines; contact the department.

### Clemson University — Mathematical Sciences

January 15 recommended for fall; later completed applications may still be considered. No year is stated. International applicants have additional timing requirements.

### Colorado School of Mines — Applied Mathematics and Statistics

Priority: November 15 international; December 15 domestic. Table: final March 1 international, June 1 domestic; introductory text more broadly refers to June 1. No cycle year is stated. Confirm your applicable final date with Mines.

### Colorado State University — Mathematics

Fall admission only; February 1 for a complete application, including supporting documents. The page does not explicitly state the entry year.

### Columbia University — Mathematics

GSAS table: December 2, 2026 for Mathematics PhD. Distinct from the Mathematics of Finance MA.

### Cornell University — Applied Mathematics

University catalog: December 15. CAM department admissions: January 7. Neither states a year. Neither is treated as confirmed for Fall 2027. Contact appliedmath@cornell.edu before setting a date.

### Cornell University — Mathematics

Department explicitly identifies Fall 2027 and December 15, 2026 for all materials. Missing or late recommendations may prevent review. Applied Mathematics is separate.

### CUNY Graduate Center — Mathematics

Program and admissions table agree on January 1 for fall, with no spring entry. The mathematics entries do not explicitly name Fall 2027.

### Dartmouth College — Mathematics

Official applicant information page: December 15, 2026 for all materials. Its last-modified footer is older than the deadline text; check the linked Guarini application for subsequent changes.

## Comparison metrics added in v0.6

Primary pages checked November 6, 2026. All 25 records can be compared on existing catalogue fields. Additional metrics are deliberately limited to verified values for seven programs, with absent values labeled “Not verified.” No funding guarantee is treated as average time to degree. - Brown Mathematics: [graduate overview](https://mathematics.brown.edu/graduate-program) (~40+ graduate students); [handbook](https://mathematics.brown.edu/graduate/graduate-program-handbook) (at least two TA semesters). Combined faculty/postdoc totals are not used as faculty counts.

- Carnegie Mellon: [graduate overview](https://www.cmu.edu/math/grad/index.html) (~35 faculty and 45–50 graduate students); [PhD requirements](https://www.cmu.edu/math/grad/phd/requirements.html) (TA and teaching-preparation requirements). Census year unspecified.
- Clarkson: [Mathematics PhD](https://www.clarkson.edu/academics/majors-minors/mathematics-phd) (minimum three academic years full-time; full TA appointment up to 20 hours/week on average). Minimum duration is not an average; appointment workload is not a universal requirement.
- Cornell Applied: [PhD timetable](https://cam.cornell.edu/academics/phd-program-timetable/) (department reports five years as average; cohort and reporting period not specified).
- Cornell Mathematics: [catalog](https://courses.cornell.edu/programs/mathematics-phd/) (most take 5–6 years, a typical range); [people overview](https://math.cornell.edu/people) (43 tenured/tenure-track faculty; 80 graduate students including visiting/non-degree students; census year unspecified).

These manually reviewed source snapshots are not a live census and should be reconfirmed with departments when making decisions.

## Local Department Drafts — v0.10

Department heads can maintain an attributed, unverified local overlay on their assigned program. These edits are not part of the official university source audit above. The UI preserves the original source records and labels department-supplied information as local drafts with email verification pending. They may override displayed program information and fee comparisons, but cannot establish verified deadlines or alter student planning dates automatically. The fictional test university remains separate from the 25 AMS-selected programs.
