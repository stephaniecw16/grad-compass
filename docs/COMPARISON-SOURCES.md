# Expanded comparison source audit

Official university/department sources checked November 6–7, 2026. Each new fact has a check date, evidence type and reporting-period label in the app. Earlier sourced facts retain November 6. Undated pages do not become a 2026 census merely because accessed in 2026.

Coverage: 149 sourced comparison details across all 25 programs, in addition to location, deadline and fee records. Every program has research, funding and degree-requirement information. Selected admissions notes, counts and timelines are included where verified. Missing comparable values stay **Not verified**.

Expected/minimum timelines, maximum funding periods and measured averages are separate. BYU’s reported average is in a post-master’s context; Cornell Applied’s average has no cohort/reporting period. Assistantship slots are not enrollment, and consortial/combined department figures are scoped explicitly. Funding summaries are conditional on the published policy and individual offer. Research summaries are selected, not exhaustive.

The table below indexes the evidence without duplicating the full app descriptions. See `src/catalog/comparison-research.js` and `src/catalog/comparison-data.js` for the corresponding paraphrases.
